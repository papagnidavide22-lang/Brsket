# Code of conduct

This repository is where people report bugs, suggest features and help each other
with Brsket. To keep it useful and welcoming:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, here or in private
  messages started from here.
- Stay on topic and keep reports factual: what you did, what you expected, what happened.
- Don't post other people's private information, or your own (private links, cookies,
  logs with personal paths: remove them first).
- Don't ask for help downloading content you have no right to download.

Issues or comments that break these rules may be edited, hidden or locked, and
repeated abuse leads to a block. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, open an
issue titled "Conduct" or use GitHub's "Report content" menu.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
